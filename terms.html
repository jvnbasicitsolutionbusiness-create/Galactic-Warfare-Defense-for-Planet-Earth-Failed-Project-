TERMS OF PLAY

Use the game lawfully and do not attempt to disrupt its services, access another player's account, or alter online progression data.

Accounts and saves

Registered play uses an email address and password. Progress and settings are saved to the configured Google Sheets storage when the service is reachable and restored to your account after you sign in. Clearing browser data removes the saved login, not the registered account's cloud save. Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

Guest play is a limited demo. Guest progress and settings are temporary and are not saved when the guest session ends.

Availability

Online account and cloud-save features depend on the game server and Google Sheets service. Local gameplay may continue during an outage, but a cloud save cannot be guaranteed until service is restored.
